# Tumbo

I build **living realities** — interactive 3D worlds you can drag, open, and play in.

## Idea archive

Every idea pass lives in **[tumbo-idea-archive](https://github.com/carltheghost/tumbo-idea-archive)** — the archaeology of how all of this came to be.

## What I'm building

- **Living Reality** — [maTumbo Living Reality](https://github.com/carltheghost/matumbo-living-reality-demo): a 3D glass-cube world — drag cubes anywhere in full 3D, double-click to travel inside them, real 3D chess (table console + full 3D avatar chess with Tumbo chibi pieces), plug-in bots, and a physics-driven fluffy Tumbo avatar (spring-physics fur, jiggle tails, squash-and-stretch landings). [Live demo](https://carltheghost.github.io/matumbo-living-reality-demo/).
- **Sports prediction** — TumboAgent (private): a six-lens convergence engine with a certified picks page — writers/creators, head-to-head, program form, transitive web, own read, plus a chain-normalized passing/possession lens.
- **Moon experiments** — [Steal the Moon](https://github.com/carltheghost/steal-the-moon): open data + specs for an interactive declassified-history story (the heist of Mimas). [Kage](https://github.com/carltheghost/kage): an interactive five-chapter night walk through a Kyoto mountain temple, rendered live in Three.js. [cinematic-moon-engine](https://github.com/carltheghost/cinematic-moon-engine): a static seeded-procedural-moon engine with atmosphere, tiered bloom, and scroll-driven chapters.
- **Writing** — [cryptoblog](https://github.com/carltheghost/cryptoblog).
- **Agency** — [ilala-amana](https://github.com/carltheghost/ilala-amana): the SuperSub agency coordinator — routes tasks to specialist intents with approval gates.

## Canonical vs historical

- **cryptoblog** is the canonical blog; **cyptoblog** is the original misspelling, kept as historical lineage.
- **matumbo-living-reality** (private) is the canonical tree; **matumbo-living-reality-demo** is the public demo mirror.
- **Reality Lens Ω** is the current name of the system-scale semantic zoom; **World Eye** is retired project-wide.

## How I work

- Vanilla JS + pinned three.js. Zero frameworks, hand-rolled deterministic math.
- Browser-viewport tested on phone and PC: zero console errors, zero mobile overflow — physical-device and XR validation are tracked separately.
- Simulated points only. Never real money or wagering.
